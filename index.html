Can I enquire if I’m a complete beginner?

Yes. Learning starts from the basics. Tell the academy you are starting from scratch so the team can recommend a suitable learning path.

What will I learn at GIFM?

Stock market fundamentals, technical and fundamental analysis, risk management and trading psychology. Ask the team for the syllabus and inclusions of your selected batch.

Are the classes live?

GIFM teaches through live online classes with real-time market examples. Ask the team about recordings and revision access for the batch you choose.

Do I need market knowledge or a lot of money to begin?

No prior knowledge is needed. You can learn the concepts first, and you should never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

What are the next batch dates and fees?

Contact the academy for the latest schedule, duration, format, language and fees. These are confirmed directly before enrolment.

How do I book a demo class?

Use any enquiry button to open the form. It prepares a WhatsApp message with your details. Send it to the academy to ask about demo availability.

Will I get help with my doubts?

Yes. Doubt-solving is part of the learning. Weekend sessions are dedicated to clarifying concepts, strategies and trades, and mentorship batches discuss live-market examples.

Do you give buy or sell tips?

No. GIFM is an educational academy. You learn how to analyse, plan your risk and decide for yourself. Nothing here is investment advice.

Do I need a demat account?

To place real trades you need a demat and trading account with a broker. You can learn the concepts first, and ask the academy for guidance when you are ready.

Does the training guarantee trading profits?

No. Education does not guarantee returns. Trading involves financial risk, including the loss of capital. Make decisions based on your circumstances and seek qualified advice where appropriate.
